# Required published news card (UI/SSR contract)

- Title original and accurate Vietnamese headline; category; importance/risk label (only if grounded).
- Publisher + original canonical URL, date original published and date verified; badges for unverified/draft hidden publicly.
- Three sentences: What changed? Who impacted? Recommended developer action?
- Claim/evidence links; if multiple sources conflicting show conflict with human review.
- Link target protected against open redirects, tracking cleaned without breaking canonical identity.
- Corrections visible; no random images scraped without license; no manipulated dates “today” based only discovered_at.
